### 🦷➡️💻 The Story

> I didn't start in software — I started in a dental clinic. A degree in **Oral & Dental Medicine** from Minia University, then a full career pivot through **ITI's Full Stack .NET program**. What survived the transition wasn't the drill — it was the discipline: diagnose precisely, plan before you cut, verify before you close.
>
> That's how I build software: **Clean Architecture**, deliberate data modeling, and a habit of checking my own work before I call it done — because in the OR and in production, "probably fine" isn't good enough.
